# Studio journal

One entry per change to the studio itself (lead contract, lanes, skills, tools, launcher): date · what (files and substance) · why (problem numbers from `studio/inventory.md`) · how to verify (which run or artifact shows the difference). Rollbacks get their own entry: "tried X, it did not work because Y". The `dev` lane reads a lane's history here before touching it and appends after every change. Newest first.
